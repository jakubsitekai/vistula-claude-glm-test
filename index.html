Exam rules
  • Do not leave the window or exit fullscreen — every leaving event is recorded as a violation.
  • Do not use the mouse until the exam is finished. Answer with the keyboard: type A, B, C or D and press Enter.
  • Answer questions one by one; Enter confirms and moves on.
  • You can finish at any time ("Finish attempt") — the score so far is kept.
